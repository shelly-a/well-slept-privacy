Delete your Well Slept account

Well Slept is a sleep debt tracker published by Shelly A. This page explains how to delete your Well Slept account and the data it holds. You only have an account if you signed in with Google in the app.

In the app

Open Well Slept, go to Profile > Account, and tap Delete account. The app asks once more, then deletes your account and your whole backup straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email wellslept.app@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with. Put "Delete my account" in the subject. We delete your account and your backup within 30 days, and reply to say it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your Google name, email address, profile picture link and account ID.
  • Your backup: every night, your sleep profile and its history, your settings and reminders, and the supplements and medication in your routine.
  • Feedback you sent while signed in.

Nothing of your account or your backup is kept on our server afterwards.

What is not deleted

  • The data on your phone. It stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Feedback you sent while signed out, or by email. It is not linked to your account. Email us to have it deleted.
  • Your Google account. Deleting your Well Slept account does not touch it.

Questions

Email wellslept.app@gmail.com. The full privacy policy is at https://shelly-a.github.io/well-slept-privacy/.
