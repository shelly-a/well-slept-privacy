Privacy Policy for Well Slept

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Well Slept is a sleep debt tracker. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your phone, and we have no way to see it.

Who we are

Well Slept is published by Shelly Amir. If you have a question about this policy or about your data, email wellslept.app@gmail.com.

We do not collect your data

Well Slept has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. Everything you enter is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage.

We do not:

  • send your sleep data anywhere
  • have a copy of it
  • share or sell it to anyone
  • use it for advertising or marketing
  • use analytics, tracking or crash reporting tools
  • show ads

Because there is no server, we cannot access your information even if you ask us to. If you delete the app, the data on that device goes with it.

What the app stores on your device

  • The sleep sessions you log or import: date, bedtime, wake time, how long you slept, time spent awake in bed, whether it was a nap, and an optional quality rating.
  • Your sleep profile: your sleep need, your wake goal, whether your wake time varies, and your age band if you gave one.
  • Your settings: which timeline windows you show, which reminders you have on, and light or dark mode.

Health Connect (Android)

Well Slept can read sleep data from Health Connect, which is where Samsung Health, Garmin Connect, Fitbit and most other trackers store the sleep they record.

  • What we read. Sleep sessions only: their start and end times and their sleep stages. Nothing else. Well Slept never writes to Health Connect.
  • When we read it. When you tap "Sync sleep now", and once a day when you open the app, but only after you have granted permission in Health Connect. Well Slept does not read in the background and does not read while you are not using the app.
  • Where it goes. Onto your device, into the same local storage as everything else. It is not transmitted anywhere.
  • What it is used for. Working out your sleep debt, your timeline and your progress. Nothing else. Health Connect data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared.
  • How to stop it. Revoke the permission in Health Connect at any time, under Health Connect > App permissions > Well Slept. Sleep already imported stays on your device until you delete the app or overwrite it with an import.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled by your phone, on your phone. Well Slept does not use push notifications, so there is no server sending you anything and no device token is created or shared. Reminders contain only a time of day and a short prompt. Turn them off in the app under Profile > Reminders, or in your phone's settings.

Sending feedback

"Send feedback" opens your own mail app with a draft to the developer. The draft includes your sleep need, how many nights you have logged, your phone's operating system and the app version, and the screen shows you all of this before your mail app opens. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you can delete any of it first.

Exporting and importing your data

"Copy export to clipboard" puts a copy of your data on your clipboard so you can save it or move it to another device. What happens to it after that is up to you, and depends on where you paste it. Well Slept does not upload it.

Importing replaces the data in the app with whatever you paste in.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, only if you turn a reminder on.
  • Health Connect sleep access, only if you use the Health Connect sync.

Well Slept asks for nothing else. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

Well Slept is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing at all, there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

Your rights

Your data is on your device and under your control. You can view all of it in the app, export it at any time, overwrite it with an import, or delete it by uninstalling the app. There is no request to make to us, because we hold nothing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and the date at the top will be updated. If a change ever means the app starts collecting something, we will say so in the app before it happens.
