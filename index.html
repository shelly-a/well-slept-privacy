Privacy Policy for Well Slept

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Well Slept is a sleep debt tracker. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in or send feedback. If you sign in, it is backed up to your account so you can get it back on a new phone. It is never sold, never used for ads, and never used for analytics.

The rules for using the app are in the terms of use.

Who we are

Well Slept is published by Shelly A. If you have a question about this policy or about your data, email wellslept.app@gmail.com.

Without an account

You do not need an account to use Well Slept. If you never sign in, everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you send feedback (see Sending feedback below). If you delete the app, the data on that device goes with it. Android can copy apps' data to your Google account. Well Slept turns that off. Some phones still copy apps' data when you move straight from one phone to another.

If you sign in: backup

Signing in is optional. You sign in with your Google account from Profile > Account. That screen lists what is backed up before you sign in, and nothing is sent until you do.

What is backed up

  • Your Google account details: your name, email address and profile picture link, and an account ID. Google gives us these when you sign in, and they are how you sign in again.
  • Your nights: the date, bedtime, wake time, how long you slept, time spent awake in bed, whether it was a nap, and how rested you felt. This includes nights read from Health Connect, along with the ID Health Connect gave each one and when your tracker last changed it. If you changed a night your tracker recorded, the tracker's version is kept with it, so you can go back to it. It also includes nights you confirmed from a sleep guess. Guesses you have not confirmed are never backed up.
  • Your sleep profile: your sleep need, whether you set it yourself, every change to it, your wake goal, and whether your wake time varies.
  • Your settings: which timeline windows you show and which reminders you have on.
  • Your routine: the supplements and anything else you have added, with the dose as you wrote it, when you take them, whether with food, whether they remind you, and the colour and icon you chose.
  • When each of these last changed. For a night you delete, only its ID and when you deleted it are kept, so the deletion reaches your other phones. The night itself is removed from the backup.

What is not backed up: your Health Connect connection and permissions, whether the sleep guess is on, sleep guesses you have not confirmed, light or dark mode, the app language, and anything else that only describes one phone.

When. When you open the app, and a few seconds after you change something, while you are signed in. Never in the background.

Where. The backup is stored by Supabase, the database service this app uses, on servers in the European Union. Supabase processes it only to run the backup for us, under a data processing agreement. It is encrypted when it travels between your phone and the server, and while it is stored.

Technical records. Like most online services, Supabase keeps some technical records when the app connects to it. With each sign-in it keeps the internet address you signed in from. That record goes when you delete your account. Its request logs, which can also show internet addresses, are kept for a short time. We do not use any of this to work out who or where you are.

Who can see it. Your account can read only its own backup. No other user can see it. We do not read your backup, except when you ask us to help with a problem with your account, when it is needed to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it.

What it is used for. Only to keep a copy of your data and give it back to you, on this phone or another one. It is never used for advertising, marketing or analytics, never used to decide anything about insurance, credit or employment, and never sold or shared with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept. For as long as your account exists. Signing out stops new backups. It leaves your data on your phone and your backup in your account, so you can sign in again later.

Deleting your account

  • In the app. Go to Profile > Account and tap Delete account. It deletes your account and your whole backup from our server straight away.
  • Without the app. Email wellslept.app@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply to say it is done. The steps are also at https://shelly-a.github.io/well-slept-privacy/delete-account.html.
  • What is deleted. Your Google account details as listed above, everything in your backup, and any feedback you sent while signed in. Nothing of your account is kept on our server afterwards.
  • What stays. The data on your phone. It stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Deleting your Well Slept account does not touch your Google account.

Health Connect (Android)

Well Slept can read sleep data from Health Connect, which is where Samsung Health, Garmin Connect, Fitbit and most other trackers store the sleep they record.

  • What we read. Sleep sessions only: their start and end times and their sleep stages. Nothing else. Well Slept never writes to Health Connect.
  • When we read it. When you connect it, when you tap "Sync now" or "Bring back deleted nights", and once a day when you open the app, but only after you have granted permission in Health Connect. Well Slept does not read Health Connect in the background and does not read it while you are not using the app.
  • Where it goes. Onto your device, into the same storage as everything else. If you sign in, these nights are included in your backup like the nights you log yourself, so they come back on a new phone. We do not send them anywhere else.
  • What it is used for. Working out your sleep debt, your timeline and your progress, and, if you sign in, keeping them in your backup. Nothing else. Health Connect data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared.
  • How to stop it. Revoke the permission in Health Connect at any time, under Health Connect > App permissions > Well Slept. Sleep already imported stays in the app, and in your backup if you are signed in, until you delete it.

Sleep guess from your phone (Android)

If you do not wear a tracker, Well Slept can guess when you slept from your phone. This is off until you turn it on. Before you do, the app shows you what it reads and asks for the Physical activity permission.

  • How it works. Android's activity recognition, part of Google Play services, uses your phone's motion and light sensors to spot when you were probably asleep. It does this on your phone. Well Slept never sees the sensor readings. It gets only the start and end time of each likely sleep.
  • When it arrives. Android sends the guess once a day, after you wake up. It can arrive while the app is closed. This is the only thing Well Slept receives in the background.
  • Where it goes. A guess is saved on your phone only. It is not backed up, not included in your export, and not sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.
  • What happens next. The next time you open the app, it asks you to check the guess. If you save it, with or without changing the times, it becomes a night like one you log yourself. If you are signed in, that night is backed up. If your tracker later sends its own record of the same sleep through Health Connect, that record replaces the saved guess. If you dismiss the guess, it is deleted. A guess you do not check is deleted after 7 days.
  • What it is used for. Only to suggest a night for you to save. A guess never counts toward your sleep debt until you save it.
  • How to stop it. Turn it off in Profile > Sleep guess. You can also remove the Physical activity permission in your phone's settings. Either way, guesses you have not checked are deleted. Nights you already saved stay until you delete them.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled by your phone, on your phone. Well Slept does not use push notifications, so there is no server sending you anything and no device token is created or shared. A reminder contains a time of day and a short prompt. The bedtime one can show your sleep need, and one for something in your routine shows its name, and the dose and timing you gave it. Depending on your phone's settings, reminders can show on the lock screen. Turn them off in the app under Profile > Reminders, or in your phone's settings.

Sending feedback

"Send feedback" lets you write to the developer from inside the app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. The screen says that phone and app details go too, and this section lists them.

  • What is sent. Your message, and your name and email address only if you type them in. Both are optional. With it go your phone's model, its operating system and version, the app version and the app's language, so a problem can be matched to the phone and build you have. Nothing about your sleep or your routine is attached. Anything you write in the message yourself is sent as you wrote it. If you are signed in, your account ID goes with it so a reply can reach you.
  • Where it goes. To the same Supabase database as the backup, in the European Union. Only the developer reads it, in Israel. The app itself cannot read it back, not even your own message. When a message arrives, the developer's phone gets an alert that says only that there is feedback.
  • Stopping spam. To limit how many messages can come from one connection, the server keeps a scrambled, one-way version of your internet connection's address for one day, then deletes it. The address itself is never stored with your message.
  • If it cannot be sent. With no connection, your message is kept on your phone and sent the next time you open the app.
  • How long it is kept. Until the developer has dealt with it, and at most a year. Feedback sent while signed in is deleted with your account. To have any feedback deleted sooner, email wellslept.app@gmail.com.
  • Email instead. "Email instead" opens your own mail app with a draft to the developer, with the same phone and app details filled in. Nothing is sent unless you press send there, and you can delete any of it first. It arrives in a Gmail inbox, so Google handles it under its own privacy policy. To have an email deleted, ask us.

Exporting and restoring your data

In Profile > Your data, you can export a copy of your data, to keep or to move to another phone. "Save as a file" saves it where you choose. "Copy as text" puts it on your clipboard. Either way, what happens to it after that is up to you, and depends on where you save, share or paste it. Well Slept does not upload the export.

Restoring replaces the data in the app with the file you choose or the text you paste in. If you are signed in, what you restore is then backed up too.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, only if you turn a reminder on.
  • Health Connect sleep access, only if you use the Health Connect sync.
  • Physical activity, only if you turn on the sleep guess (Android 10 and later).
  • Run at startup (Android), so your reminders and the sleep guess keep working after your phone restarts or the app updates. For the sleep guess, it does nothing unless the sleep guess is on.

Signing in uses your Google account through Android's own sign-in screen. Well Slept never sees your Google password. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts. It does not look through your files. It opens or saves only an export file you choose.

Age

Well Slept is for adults, aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has signed in or sent feedback, email us and we will delete their account, backup and messages.

Your rights

Without an account, your data is on your device and under your control. You can view all of it in the app, export it at any time, replace it by restoring an export, or delete it by uninstalling the app.

If you sign in, you can see everything in your backup in the app, since it is the same data. You can export it, correct it by changing it in the app, and delete it as described above. Wherever you live, you can email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Many countries give you rights like these by law. We give them to everyone.

If you are in the EU or UK (GDPR)

The GDPR is the data protection law of the European Union. The UK has its own version. They apply when you use Well Slept from there, and because your backup is stored in the EU. This section covers what they ask us to tell you.

Who is responsible for your data. Shelly A., the developer of Well Slept, based in Israel. Email wellslept.app@gmail.com about anything to do with your data.

Why we may use your data (the legal basis)

  • Backup: your consent. Your nights and routine count as health information, which the GDPR treats as sensitive. That is why you tick a box saying you agree, before you can sign in.
  • Feedback: our legitimate interest in answering you and fixing problems in the app. You choose to send it.
  • The scrambled address that stops spam: our legitimate interest in keeping the feedback service working. It is deleted after one day.
  • Technical records: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.
  • Account deletion requests: the law requires us to act on them. We keep a record of your request only as long as we need to show it was done.

Who processes it for us. Supabase stores the backup and feedback in the EU, under a data processing agreement, using its own approved service providers. Google handles signing in, under Google's own privacy policy. Google Play also shows us figures about installs and crashes, which Google collects under its own privacy policy. We do not see who they are about, unless you write to us through Google Play. No one else receives your data from us.

Data outside the EU. Your backup and your feedback are stored in the EU. We read feedback in Israel. If we read your backup, for example to help with your account, that happens in Israel too. The European Commission and the UK recognise Israel as protecting personal data to the same standard. If we ever move the backup to another provider or another country, we will update this policy and tell you in the app first, and use the safeguards the law requires.

Your rights. You can ask us to:

  • show you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop or limit how we use it
  • give it to you in a file you can take elsewhere. The export in Profile > Your data does this.

You can take back your consent at any time by deleting your account. Email us for any of the above and we will reply within one month.

No automatic decisions. Well Slept never makes decisions about you that have legal or similar effects.

If you are not happy. Please email us first, so we can put it right. You also have the right to contact the data protection authority in your country.
